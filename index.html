Yueyan Liu

Research Assistant, The Future Laboratory, Tsinghua University

I study how people experience, adapt to, and negotiate emerging intelligent systems in everyday life and work. My research spans smart homes, human-centered AI, aging and care, cultural heritage experiences, and user experience research.
